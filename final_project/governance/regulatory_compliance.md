# Regulatory Compliance Summary

## GDPR
Potentially relevant when personal data of people in the EEA is processed. The project design supports purpose limitation, data minimization, security, retention controls, transparency, and data-subject rights processes. A real deployment would require legal determination of lawful basis, controller/processor roles and any automated-decision obligations.

## CCPA / CPRA
Potentially relevant depending on organization and California consumer-data scope. A real implementation should support notice, access/deletion/correction where applicable, purpose controls, retention limits and appropriate handling of sharing/sale definitions.

## HIPAA
Not applicable to the current entertainment/customer-churn dataset because the project does not process protected health information. If health information were later introduced, applicability would need a new legal and governance assessment.

## Governance framework
NIST AI RMF is used as a voluntary structure for Govern, Map, Measure and Manage activities. This document is an academic compliance mapping, not legal advice or a declaration of regulatory compliance.
